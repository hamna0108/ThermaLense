Diagnostic Mechanism on High-Noise Detectability (sigma = 0.05):
  - When alpha0 must be estimated simultaneously from the same window that contains the jump,
    the single-diffusion null fit shifts towards alpha1, soaking up part of the residual drop.
  - In the semi-oracle test, even with exact tc and jump factor m known, estimating alpha0 under test
    reduces power from 76-86% down to 37-57%, isolating nuisance-parameter absorption as the dominant effect.
  - When alpha0 is anchored by a separate healthy baseline window, Class C recall surges to 58-70%,
    recovering the majority of the gap and demonstrating that the limitation is parameter unidentifiability
    under joint estimation rather than a failure of the continuous GLR formulation.
