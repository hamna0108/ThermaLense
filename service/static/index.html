Know the limits

Key physical and operational boundaries to know before operating the twin:

Read all operational limits →
  • Assumed edge temperature & initial profile: Standard models assume the bar edges stay at a fixed zero temperature and heat starts in a pure sine shape; custom boundaries or non-standard heating require declared physics or the twin will flag invalidity.
  • At least 20 normal baseline readings: The fault detector needs at least 20 healthy sensor readings to establish a reliable noise benchmark; requests with fewer readings cause high false alarms and are strictly refused by the service.
  • Verified thermal diffusivity range: Neural surrogate calculations are validated for thermal diffusivity α between 0.01 and 1.0; outside this range the system automatically routes to the classical numerical solver to avoid extrapolation errors.
Technical details & Mathematical formulation

Governing Equations & Boundary Conditions

ThermaLens models unsteady one-dimensional thermal diffusion in a homogeneous bar governed by the parabolic partial differential equation ∂u/∂t = α ∂²u/∂x² on the spatial domain x ∈ [0, 1] and temporal interval t ∈ [0, 1], subject to homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions u(0, t) = u(1, t) = 0 and fundamental harmonic initial condition u(x, 0) = sin(πx). For two-dimensional steady-state heat conduction, the twin pairs this unsteady diffusion process with a Poisson elliptic operator −∇²u = f(x, y) on the unit square [0, 1]² with distributed source heating.

Neural Surrogate Hierarchy & Numerical Solver Ground Truth

Rather than relying on isolated neural black boxes, the architecture implements a hybrid hierarchy where the Fourier Neural Operator (FNO) operates as an accelerated surrogate and the classical numerical solver serves as the ground-truth physical reference. FNO is not faster than the solver for field generation here; its primary value is providing a fast surrogate grid inside parameter inversion (Mode 3 ~0.7 ms/fit). For parameter inversion, the Mode 3+ live Crank-Nicolson polish nominal latency for the default case (layout: N=10 log-spaced sensors t ∈ [0.01, 1.0], x ∈ [0.2, 0.8], α=0.10, σ=0.01) is ~58.4 ms (service endpoint) / ~49.3 ms (direct polish), with 138.8 ms mean over all 135 benchmark cells including stiff out-of-distribution regimes. A bounded reparametrized Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN Exp D) provides analytical autograd parameter gradients ∂L/∂α without spatial meshing.

Mandatory Caveats & Honest Findings

        
    
          
  • Mode 3 vs Mode 3+ Coverage Trade-off: Mode 3 achieves 88.2% mean Wald coverage (dropping to 61.8% on Extended High-α OOD) in ~0.7 ms. Mode 3+ hybrid polish recovers 94.0% coverage across all 135 parameter cells: nominal latency for the default case (layout: N=10 log-spaced sensors t ∈ [0.01, 1.0], x ∈ [0.2, 0.8], α=0.10, σ=0.01) is ~58.4 ms (service endpoint) / ~49.3 ms (direct CN polish), with 138.8 ms labelled "mean over all 135 cells incl. stiff OOD".
    • 
          
  • Classical Solver Speed Superiority for Field Generation: FNO is NOT faster than the solver for field generation here. Measured at 101×201 resolution on this machine, the numerical solver executes in 7.3–18.6 ms (p50: 7.3 ms, p95: 18.0–18.6 ms) vs FNO in 134–166 ms (p50: 134–144 ms, p95: 145–166 ms). In 2D Poisson, the pre-factorized sparse direct LU solver solves in 0.50 ms vs 20.5 ms for FNO inference. FNO's primary value is precomputing continuous surrogate grids for sub-millisecond inverse parameter estimation (Mode 3 ~0.7 ms/fit).
    • 
          
  • Missed V2 Stress Targets & Final State: D2 (GOF Gate) final false-flag rate is 4.2% for N=10 (before: 7.0% in preliminary 100-run sample; 5.4% for N=5, 5.9% for N=20). AR(1) correlated noise is detected via Durbin-Watson at 23.9% (ρ=0.5), 60.4% (ρ=0.8), and 80.5% (ρ=0.95). D3 (Range Guard) target 95% is missed at 90.0% [82.56, 94.48] under the new multi-criteria rule (before: 72.0% preliminary, 65.0% old 1% rule; 100% routed to Mode 3+). D5 drift warning has no power (3.3/4.2/5.6% vs 3.8% false warnings); kurtosis warning achieves 10–34%.
    • 
          
  • High-Frequency Operator Failure: On Poisson wavenumbers m, n > 4, FNO median relative L₂ error jumps to 80.4%, while linear baseline remains at 1.5%.

Operational Boundaries & Mathematical Limits

        
Clear, honest statement of conditions where the digital twin cannot or should not be deployed, linked directly to verified empirical evidence.

      


      

        
What the Digital Twin Cannot Do

        

          

            
              

                Boundary Condition
                Operational Constraint & Mathematical Cause
                Empirical Evidence Link
              

            
            
              

                Assumed Physics & BCs
                The standard twin assumes homogeneous Dirichlet boundaries u = 0 and fundamental harmonic initial state sin(πx). Under unannounced boundary offsets or sources, the goodness-of-fit gate correctly flags invalidity, but parameter estimates degrade without declared physics.
                Evidence Section 4 & 5 (S4–S6, D4)
              

              

                Baseline Requirement (k ≥ 20)
                Noise estimation σ̂ and GLR baseline calibration fail statistically below 20 readings. Healthy false alarm rates exceed 15% for k ≤ 5. The service strictly refuses requests with k < 20.
                Evidence Section 3 (Table 2)
              

              

                Diffusivity Range Limits
                The FNO neural surrogate is verified only for α ∈ [0.01, 1.0]. Outside this range, the Range Guard routes queries to Mode 3+ or the numerical solver; severe extrapolation errors occur if FNO is forced.
                Evidence Section 1 & 5 (S12, D3)
              

              

                Correlated & Heavy-Tailed Noise
                Wald confidence intervals assume independent Gaussian perturbations. Strongly correlated AR(1) noise and Cauchy heavy-tailed noise invalidate the Fisher information matrix and breach the 80% coverage threshold.
                Evidence Section 4 (S2, S3)
              

              

                Slow Thermal Baseline Drift
                Uncompensated sensor calibration drift over time masquerades as anomalous physical diffusion or triggers false alarms in the GLR cascade unless periodically recalibrated against fresh baseline runs.
                Evidence Section 4 (S13)
              

              

                Poisson High-Mode Operator Failure
                The Fourier Neural Operator fails on 2D Poisson source terms with harmonic wavenumbers m, n > 4, exhibiting 80.4% median relative error. Only the classical sparse direct solver or linear baseline should be deployed for high-frequency loading.
                Evidence Section 6 (High Modes)
              

              

                Simulation vs Hardware Domain Gap
                All validation datasets, noise models, and sensor faults are synthesized via numerical solutions. No hardware test bench with physical thermocouples or real materials has been evaluated.
                Evidence Section 7 (Meta)
